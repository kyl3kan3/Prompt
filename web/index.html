What leaves this machine — exactly

Your archive files are read in place and are never uploaded anywhere. To analyze them, sampled excerpts (at most 150 lines from each of at most 200 sessions) are sent to the Anthropic API under your own API key, after a local redaction pass masks secrets and replaces emails/IPs with placeholders. Your key's data-retention policy applies to those excerpts. Redaction catches structured secrets (keys, tokens, connection strings) but cannot reliably catch secrets written as plain prose. A local audit log records everything that was read and every redaction that fired.
